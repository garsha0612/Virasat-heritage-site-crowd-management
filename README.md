# 🏛️ Heritage Site Crowd Management System

A Smart Tourism & Crowd Management Platform

## 📌 Overview

The **Heritage Site Crowd Management System** is a smart tourism platform designed to improve visitor experiences and manage crowd flow at popular heritage destinations such as the Taj Mahal, historical monuments, museums, forts, and other major tourist attractions.

The platform provides visitors with real-time information about tourist destinations, historical details, ticket prices, expected crowd levels, available time slots, and live entry statistics.

It aims to reduce overcrowding, minimize waiting times, improve visitor safety, and support efficient management of heritage sites through a technology-driven solution.

## ✨ Key Features

- **Heritage Site Information:** Explore famous historical monuments, museums, forts, and tourist destinations with detailed descriptions and historical backgrounds.
- **Smart Ticket Booking:** View ticket prices, ticket categories, and booking information.
- **Crowd Prediction:** Check expected crowd levels using historical visitor data and current statistics.
- **Time-Slot Availability:** View available entry slots and plan visits accordingly.
- **Live Crowd Statistics:** Monitor visitor entry and exit statistics through an interactive dashboard.
- **Crowd Level Indicators:** Display crowd conditions such as Low, Moderate, High, and Very High.
- **Interactive Dashboard:** Present important visitor statistics and crowd insights through charts and visualizations.
- **Responsive Design:** Access the platform seamlessly across desktops, tablets, and mobile devices.
